# Soft Kettlebell — video format (current)

Applies to every new edit. Each project folder (`video/`, `kelebihan-v2/`, `gelebeh-peha/`, `lemak-peha/`,
`perut-buncit/`, `perut-buncit-vo/`) is a HyperFrames project generated by its `tools/build.py`.

## Picture
- 1080×1920, 60 fps, built with HyperFrames. Max 10 clips; intro is one shot at normal speed and not reused from the previous task.
- Every clip: short zoom-in (1.00 → 1.08, 0.55 s) and a white flash on the cut.
- Feature / exercise scenes use real footage of that exact exercise (check the movement, not the file name).
- iPhone HDR (HLG) footage is tone-mapped to SDR BT.709 in `tools/prep_footage.sh`; render with `--sdr`.

## Text
- Anton for titles/captions, Montserrat for labels.
- **Top headline 150 px, sub-headline 70 px** (Anton), from y ≈ 318 in the top band (x 120–888); long headlines split
  over two 150 px lines rather than shrinking. Labels/pills stay Montserrat 36 px. (Older projects used 140/112/84.)
- Captions 60 px, uppercase, black stroke, active word yellow, keywords neon green, 2–3 words, centre-bottom.
- No overlap: when a graphic shows a clause, that clause has no caption.
- No "Kettlebell di rumah" tag on the hook. Fact images only from free sources (Gray's Anatomy, PubChem, Wikimedia with credit).

## Sound
- No music. Voiceover untouched (100 %, no cuts), checked with `tools/verify_vo_sync.py` (0 ms lag).
- **Footage original sound: every clip at -57 LUFS** in the mix (≈ 27 dB under a -30 LUFS VO) —
  `tools/level_footage.py` (copy from `../tools_level_footage.py`): louder clips turned down, quieter clips turned
  up by at most +12 dB. Footage whose sound has music is muted.
- SFX synthesised (`tools/sfx.py`: whoosh, pop, ding, impact, cha-ching, riser, zap), low, under the VO.

## CTA
- Button "Link / BEG KUNING / kat bawah ni!" (or "Grab kettlebell kat beg kuning sekarang!" for no-VO videos),
  arrows on the far left, final frame checked.

## Delivery
- Render master, then a 2-pass H.264 delivery at ~11 Mbps (≈ 70 MB for 50 s). Check snapshots of every scene first.
